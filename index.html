Records are stored in the shared Firestore database. Last backup: never. Recommended: back up at the end of every shift/day and keep copies on a network drive or USB.

Restore from JSON

Every record is validated first. Merge only adds records that do not exist. Replace is staged: current data is downloaded, all backup records are written, and only then are records missing from the backup deleted; a failure during writing is undone automatically.
